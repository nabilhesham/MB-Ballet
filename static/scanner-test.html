Reading this

Max gap under 40ms with a clean payload means the scanner is in keyboard mode and working. Set GAP_MS in static/reception.html to roughly three times the max gap you see here.

Max gap above 100ms means the scanner emits slowly. Raise GAP_MS, or the receptionist typing will be mistaken for scans.

Red characters below the payload are outside A–Z and 2–7. If you see them the keyboard layout is remapping the scanner — switch Windows to English and scan again to confirm.

Nothing appears at all: the scanner is in serial mode. Scan the USB-KBW barcode in its manual to switch it to keyboard mode.
